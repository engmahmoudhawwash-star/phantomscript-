About the Project

Exploring the idea of what remains undiscovered.

Project Origin

A school assignment turned into an imaginative deep-ocean journey.

The Unknown 80% was created as a school project focused on the wonders and mysteries of the deep ocean. The project explores the idea that most of the ocean remains unknown to humans, and that the unexplored regions below the surface still hold countless possibilities for discovery.

The project began as a group assignment, where the goal was to create a presentation that could communicate a concept in a more memorable and engaging way. Instead of relying only on a traditional paper or simple slideshow, the team decided to make the project more interactive and immersive.

Project Goal

  • Bring science, storytelling, and creativity together
  • Present a fictional deep-ocean expedition
  • Build a website that feels informative and engaging
  • Combine submarine design, narrative, and presentation into one experience

What Makes It Different

Storytelling, model-making, and website design in one concept.

Storytelling

The project uses a fictional adventure to explain the excitement, danger, and wonder of underwater exploration.

Submarine Model

The AE-7 submarine is a central element of the project and was designed and built by the creator as part of the assignment.

“The project was originally completed by a group of five students, and the creator designed and built the AE-7 submarine model for the presentation. The website brings that work to life in a more interactive and accessible format.”
