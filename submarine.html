AE-7 Submarine

Built for the unknown.

Overview

AE-7 is a fictional deep-ocean exploration vehicle.

AE-7 is a fictional deep-ocean exploration vehicle designed to reach unknown ocean regions that remain beyond everyday human exploration. Its purpose is to investigate hidden areas of the sea, gather information, and help create a story framework for discovery, survival, and scientific curiosity.

The design is intended to show speed, manoeuvrability and durability. It is built to move through challenging underwater conditions, with rear rocket thrusters helping support movement and rapid navigation in the deep sea.

Confirmed Systems

Core systems designed for long-distance underwater missions.

Rear Rocket Thrusters

Purpose: Speed, movement, manoeuvrability.

Food Storage

Purpose: Long-duration missions.

Water Storage & Filtration

Purpose: Remove salt from seawater.

Oxygen Extraction System

Purpose: Converts water resources into breathable oxygen.

Control Screens

Purpose: Navigation, mission information, and story interaction.
