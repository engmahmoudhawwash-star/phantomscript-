Deep-Ocean Discovery

THE UNKNOWN 80%

Exploring the mysteries hidden beneath the ocean.

This project is a school-based exploration of the vast, unseen world beneath the ocean’s surface. It celebrates scientific curiosity, adventure, and the idea that the sea still holds secrets waiting to be discovered. At the centre of this story is the fictional deep-ocean exploration submarine AE-7, a vehicle designed to venture into the unknown and uncover what remains hidden from humanity.

Through storytelling, model-building, and an interactive website, the project presents a fictional expedition that captures both the wonder of the deep sea and the risks of exploring places few have ever reached.
