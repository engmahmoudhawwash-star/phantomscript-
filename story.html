Story Line

A two-part underwater adventure.

Part 1

THE RESEARCHER'S EXPEDITION

  1. Researcher hesitates over waiver
  2. Researcher approves waiver
  3. Receives a huge submarine
  4. Begins a two-year expedition
  5. Searches for new species
  6. Megalodon attacks submarine
  7. Researcher miraculously survives
  8. Five years pass
  9. Researcher becomes a hero
  10. Researcher ends up in hospital in a coma

Part 2

The Deep Awakens

The second chapter continues the adventure beyond the first expedition and focuses on the consequences of the encounter, the mystery behind the unexplored ocean, and the lasting mark the journey leaves on the researcher. The story expands the idea that the sea still hides more than humanity can fully understand.
